A bit more about how I work.

Engineer turned PM. Worked in deep-tech at Log9, in enterprise storage at Dell, in fintech at Rang De, and now technical PM at Akteena. If there's a thread: I tend to end up wherever the process is slow, manual or breaking — and rebuild the system around it.

principles // non-negotiable, but quietly

01
Start at the slow step.
Before the spec, I try to figure out where the actual constraint is. Sometimes it's an obvious bottleneck; sometimes it's a thing nobody's measuring. The spec follows.
02
Build the system, not just the feature.
A loan-review tool is a tool. A loan-review system that's measurably faster and cheaper is what tends to stick.
03
Instrument before launch, not after.
If we can't see the metric move on a dashboard, I'm not really sure if we changed anything. The measurement goes in first.
